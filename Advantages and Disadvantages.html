Healthy Play Builder

Advantages

  • Encourages physical fitness and active lifestyle.
  • Improves mental health and reduces stress.
  • Helps in developing teamwork and social skills.
  • Reduces screen time among children.
  • Promotes creativity and problem-solving abilities.

Disadvantages

  • Requires proper supervision for younger children.
  • Limited accessibility in small or indoor spaces.
  • May need equipment or open play areas.
  • Not suitable for all age groups or abilities.
  • Time management can be challenging for busy families.
